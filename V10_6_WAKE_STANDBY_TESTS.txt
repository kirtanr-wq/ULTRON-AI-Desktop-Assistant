ULTRON V10.6 — WAKE + STANDBY TESTS

Expected startup:
- Terminal prints STATE> STANDBY
- Ultron says NOTHING aloud.

TEST 1 — Ignore speech while asleep
Say: open YouTube
Expected: ignored; no action, no spoken reply.

TEST 2 — Wake only
Say: Ultron
Expected: STATE> ACTIVE and Ultron says: Yes?

TEST 3 — Active conversation
Say: open YouTube
Expected: YouTube opens without repeating the wake word.

TEST 4 — Enter standby
Say: go on standby mode
Expected: Ultron says: Standing by. Then STATE> STANDBY.

TEST 5 — Ignore ordinary speech again
Say: open Google
Expected: ignored; no action.

TEST 6 — Wake with a command in one sentence
Say: Ultron, open Google
Expected: STATE> ACTIVE and Google opens.

TEST 7 — Permanent shutdown while active
Say: shut down / exit Ultron (use whichever existing exit phrase routes to exit)
Expected: application exits normally.
